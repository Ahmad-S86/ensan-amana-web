Ethical Practice & Trust

Our Commitment to Accountability and Safeguarding

Al Ensan Amana Association is committed to delivering its work with integrity, respect, accountability, and a strong focus on the safety and dignity of the people and communities it serves.

Key Principles

Institutional Commitments

Our six core pillars ensuring safety, fairness, and responsible management across all activities.

Code of Conduct

We expect respectful, ethical, and professional conduct from everyone involved in our work.

Safeguarding and Protection

We are committed to protecting children, women, vulnerable persons, staff, volunteers, and community members from harm and abuse.

Prevention of Exploitation, Abuse and Harassment

We maintain a zero-tolerance approach to exploitation, abuse, harassment, discrimination, fraud, and corruption.

Inclusion and Non-Discrimination

We promote fair and respectful access to activities and services without discrimination.

Responsible Management

We apply responsible administrative and financial practices to support accountability and appropriate use of resources.

Transparency and Learning

We value documentation, feedback, continuous improvement, and responsible engagement with communities and stakeholders.

Further Policy Inquiries

For further information about organizational policies or collaboration requirements, please contact the Association directly.
