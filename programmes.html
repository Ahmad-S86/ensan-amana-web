Community Initiatives

Programmes and Activities

Our activities are shaped by community priorities and designed to support participation, wellbeing, learning, inclusion, and access to practical assistance.

Key Themes

Core Activity Themes

Comprehensive initiatives built around the evolving needs of families and individuals.

Educational Support

Supporting children and families through learning materials, school-related assistance, literacy support, and education-focused activities.

Child and Youth Activities

Creating opportunities for children and young people to learn, participate, develop skills, and engage in positive recreational and cultural activities.

Community Health Awareness

Supporting health awareness initiatives and community-based activities that promote knowledge, prevention, and access to relevant support.

Essential Support for Families

Providing practical assistance to individuals and families experiencing hardship and challenging living conditions.

Youth Employability Support

Helping young people strengthen practical skills through CV writing, communication, life skills, job-readiness guidance, mock interview preparation, and follow-up support.

Community Awareness and Outreach

Supporting awareness sessions and engagement activities that promote safer behaviours, positive participation, and stronger community connections.

Inclusion and Social Support

Supporting inclusive approaches that recognize the needs and rights of older persons, persons with disabilities, children, and other community members.
