Who We Are

Building Resilience & Opportunity Together

Al Ensan Amana Association is a Lebanese civil society organization committed to supporting individuals and communities through inclusive, community-based initiatives. We work to strengthen access to essential services, promote wellbeing and participation, and help communities build resilience in times of hardship and change.

Focus Areas

What We Do

Supporting vulnerable individuals and communities through six essential pillars of work.

Education and Learning Support

We support learning opportunities for children and young people through educational assistance, learning materials, literacy-related initiatives, and engaging activities.

Learn more

Health Awareness and Access

We contribute to community health awareness and help connect individuals and families with essential health-related support and services.

Learn more

Protection and Psychosocial Support

We promote safe, inclusive, and supportive spaces that strengthen wellbeing, protection, and social inclusion.

Learn more

Humanitarian Assistance

We provide practical assistance for individuals and families facing poverty, crisis, displacement, and difficult living conditions.

Learn more

Youth and Women’s Empowerment

We support young people and women with life skills, employability preparation, communication skills, guidance, and pathways toward greater participation and opportunity.

Learn more

Community Engagement & Capacity Building

We work with communities, local organizations, and service actors to strengthen awareness, participation, coordination, and community-led responses.

Guided By Principles

Our Values

Our commitment to respectful, ethical, and community-centered humanitarian action.

Human Dignity

We believe every person deserves respect, safety, and the opportunity to live with dignity.

Inclusion & Non-Discrimination

We seek to create accessible and inclusive opportunities for people of different backgrounds, ages, genders, and abilities.

Community Participation

We listen to communities and encourage meaningful participation in activities that affect their lives.

Accountability & Responsible Practice

We are committed to ethical conduct, responsible use of resources, and respectful engagement with communities.
