Organizational Profile

About Al Ensan Amana Association

Al Ensan Amana Association is a Lebanese non-profit civil society organization working with communities to respond to social, humanitarian, educational, health, and protection-related needs. Our approach is rooted in human dignity, inclusion, local engagement, and practical support that contributes to longer-term resilience.

Our Mission

Our mission is to support and empower individuals and communities by promoting access to essential services, strengthening participation and inclusion, and responding to humanitarian and social needs with respect, responsibility, and dignity.

Our Vision

We envision communities where children, young people, women, older persons, and persons with disabilities can live safely, access opportunities and essential support, participate meaningfully in society, and contribute to more inclusive and resilient communities.

Youth engagement and community-based activities designed to promote inclusion and wellbeing.
Guiding Operational Principles

How We Work

Five core principles shaping every initiative we undertake alongside communities.

Community-Based Engagement

We work close to communities and seek to understand local priorities, challenges, and strengths.

Partnership and Coordination

We value collaboration with local organizations, community structures, service providers, and other relevant actors.

Inclusion and Respect

We promote non-discrimination and respectful access to support for people from diverse backgrounds.

Practical Support & Resilience

We combine immediate support with activities that strengthen skills, confidence, participation, and longer-term resilience.

Safety and Accountability

We are committed to ethical practice, safeguarding, responsible management, and the protection of dignity.
